I am a faculty member in the English Department at Case Western Reserve University, teaching in the Writing Program.  My teaching and research range from interactive digital narrative and digital media studies to British colonial, post-colonial, and contemporary Anglophone literature and cultures, especially related to travel and mobility.  I'm interested in critical making practices and digital pedagogy.
