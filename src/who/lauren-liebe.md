Lauren Liebe is an assistant teaching professor of game studies and digital humanities at Penn State Behrend. They are the director of the Virtual and Augmented Reality and Interactive Arts lab, a VR, video game, and technology history space. 

Her current research is on the history and sociality of _Doom_ mods, video game adaptations of Shakespeare's _Tempest_, and the pedagogical uses of the PLATO computer system for teaching game history and game development.

Her PhD studies focused on the relationship between Restoration drama, adaptation practices, and politics.
