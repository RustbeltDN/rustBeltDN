I am professor of history and director of digital humanities at Cleveland State University. After serving 15 years as director of CSU's social studies program, I co-developed the major in [digital humanities](https://artsandsciences.csuohio.edu/history/digital-humanities) which launched in 2025. I am interested in the scholarship of teaching & learning and advocate for open access and open educational resources. As a digital humanities practitioner, I am interested in spatial history and mapping, AI literacy & pedagogy, object-based learning, and podcasting. I am a cofounder of the [Cleveland Teaching Collaborative](https://cleteaching.org/) and cohost of the [Chalk Talk with CLETeaching](https://podcast.cleteaching.org/) podcast. I am currently working with Sabine Kretzschmar to digitize, catalog and create curriculum materials for the Education Art Collection at the Cleveland Museum of Art.
