I am the Assistant Director of the Michael Schwartz Library at Cleveland State University, where I lead the Collections and Resource Management division. I oversee the development, preservation, and accessibility of the library’s physical, digital, and archival resources. I also lead our team for Cleveland Memory, a freely searchable online collection of digital photographs, texts, oral histories, videos and other local history resources created by the Michael Schwartz Library in collaboration with community partners throughout Northeast Ohio.  My professional interests include digital collections and preservation, metadata and critical cataloging, accessibility in digital environments, and scholarly communication. I teach and provide training on metadata and critical cataloging and am currently working with Shelley Rose and Sabine Kretzschmar on the cataloging component of the project to digitize, catalog and create curriculum materials for the Education Art Collection at the Cleveland Museum of Art.
