I'm Elisa Beshero-Bondar, an adventurer in the webs of Digital Humanities. I'm Professor of Digital Humanities at Penn State Erie, The Behrend College where I teach in the Digital Media, Arts, and Technology ("Digit") program. 

### Selected Projects 

#### TEI

I'm active with the [Text Encoding Initiative (TEI)](https://tei-c.org/), and enjoy helping to develop TEI projects to share cultural heritage resources. I'm also very interested in sustainability, digital data history and re-mediating digital projects and data for the future, keeping them "alive" after their software ceases to exist.  
[![Log of the Text Encoding Initiatve (TEI)](/img/members/TEI_Logo.png "TEI Logo")](https://tei-c.org/)

#### Newtfire 

You can find the DH projects I've been working on, including a lot of DH class materials woven into my website [https://newtfire.org](https://newtfire.org), named for my pet firebelly newts.
