I'm Elisa Beshero-Bondar, an adventurer in the webs of Digital Humanities known to students as "Dr. B" . I'm Professor of Digital Humanities at Penn State Erie, The Behrend College and I teach in the Digital Media, Arts, and Technology ("Digit") program. My webs are woven into [https://newtfire.org](https://newtfire.org), named for my pet firebelly newts.
